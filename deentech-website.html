USB webcam for OBS, Zoom and Teams

Your phone camera, wired into your PC.

Deenstudio streams your phone's camera over a USB cable for sharp video with very low delay. No Wi-Fi, no dropouts, no expensive webcam.

Download for WindowsDesktop app · Windows 10 / 11 · zip file Download for AndroidPhone app · Android 8.0 or newer

You need both: the phone app and the Windows app. Mac and iPhone versions are not available yet.

Up and running in three steps

The Windows app handles the connection for you. Plug in, tap start, and your phone shows up in OBS.

Install both apps

Install Deenstudio on your phone and run the Windows app on your PC. Turn on USB debugging on the phone once.

Plug in the cable

The PC app finds your phone, opens the connection and shows you a live preview. If your phone asks, tap Allow.

Tap Start stream

Add a Media Source in OBS pointing at the PC app, and you are live. Zoom and Teams work through OBS Virtual Camera.

Built for streamers and calls

Everything you need to get a clean picture without fiddling with settings.

Low latency over USB

The video travels through the cable, not your Wi-Fi, so it stays steady while your network is busy.

720p, 1080p and 4K

Pick your resolution and 30 or 60 fps. Modes your phone can't handle are greyed out.

Manual camera control

Lock focus, set exposure and choose a white balance so your picture doesn't hunt or flicker.

Front or rear camera

Use the sharper rear camera, or the front one when you want to see yourself.

Keeps running in the background

Minimise the phone app or switch the screen off and the stream carries on.

Live connection stats

A green or red status light plus bitrate, frame rate and dropped frames, right on the screen.

  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click Deenstudio.exe.
  3. If Windows shows a blue warning, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Plug in your phone and follow the on-screen steps.

Download not starting? Open the files in Google Drive: Android app · Windows app. Drive may say it can't scan the file for viruses. Click Download anyway.

Add it to OBS Studio

Keep the Deenstudio Windows app open while you stream. It sends the video to OBS for you.

  1. In OBS, under Sources, click + and choose Media Source.
  2. Untick Local File.
  3. In Input, type tcp://127.0.0.1:8001
  4. In Input Format, type h264
  5. Click OK. Your phone camera appears.
  6. For Zoom, Teams or Meet, click Start Virtual Camera in OBS and pick OBS Virtual Camera in the call app.
Tip for the lowest delay. In the Media Source, set network buffering to the minimum and untick "Close file when inactive". If the picture is upside down, right-click the source and choose Transform, then Rotate 180.

Questions

Why does my phone warn me about installing the app?

Deenstudio is installed directly from this site instead of an app store, so Android shows a standard warning about unknown sources. Allow it for this install only. You can switch the permission off again afterwards.

Why does Windows say the app is from an unknown publisher?

New apps that are not code-signed trigger Windows SmartScreen. Click More info, then Run anyway. The warning fades as more people use the app.

Google Drive says it can't scan the file for viruses. Is it safe?

Drive shows that message for any file over about 100 MB. Click Download anyway. Windows may also warn about an unknown publisher because the app is not code-signed. Only download Deenstudio from this page.

Why do I need to turn on USB debugging?

The Windows app uses it to open a private connection through the cable. Go to Settings, About phone, tap Build number seven times, then open Developer options and turn on USB debugging.

Does my video go over the internet?

No. The video only travels through the USB cable to your own computer. Deenstudio does not upload it anywhere.

Does it work on iPhone or Mac?

Not yet. Right now Deenstudio works with Android phones and Windows PCs.

Can my phone do 4K at 60 fps?

That depends on the phone. Deenstudio only enables the modes your phone supports, and 4K at 60 fps is rare. 1080p at 30 or 60 fps works well on most phones.

Something is not working. Where do I get help?

Open the Deenstudio Windows app. It shows which step is stuck (cable, permission, tunnel or video) and what to do. You can also report a problem here.
